### Judge-corrected scores

Two hidden-test false negatives affect NF only:

1. `bitemporal-entitlement`: NF passes public 4/4 and hidden 15/16, but fails
   `test_query_is_read_only_single_statement_and_reaches_kernel` because it has a
   semicolon inside a SQL comment. The query is one read-only `WITH ... SELECT`.
2. `robust-orient2d`: NF passes the public suite and hidden 6/7, but fails
   `test_uncertain_path_reaches_protected_expansion_primitives` because it uses
   supplied `scale_expansion`/`two_sum` primitives instead of importing the exact
   symbol `expansion_product`. The test raises `AttributeError` before checking
   behavior.

Correcting those two hidden tests would give:

| Metric | NF Stream | 27B Thinking |
|---|---:|---:|
| Judge-corrected attempts | **41/100** | 35/100 |
| Judge-corrected tasks | **9/20** | 8/20 |

NF's corrected task set contains every task the 27B solved, plus
`msgpack-incremental`. The 27B has no task that NF does not also solve after the
correction.

### Excluding the two judge-affected tasks

On the 18 unaffected tasks (90 attempts each):

|---|---:|---:|

The 27B's raw lead comes entirely from the two judge false negatives. On

### Genuine model differences

- **NF win:** `msgpack-incremental` — NF 4/5 vs 27B 0/5.
- **27B relative win:** `wasm-compression-wat` pass rate — 27B 4/5 vs NF 2/5;
  both solve the task.
- **NF also has higher pass rates:** `aligned-reallocator` (5/5 vs 4/5),
  `async-cache-generation` (5/5 vs 2/5).
- The remaining solved tasks are equal or effectively tied.

**Conclusion:** On the raw ledger the 27B appears slightly ahead (35 vs 31,
8/20 vs 7/20). After correcting the two verifier false negatives, NF is ahead
(41 vs 35, 9/20 vs 8/20), and NF's task set is a strict superset of the 27B's.
The 27B did not beat NF on this suite; its apparent lead is a judge artifact.
